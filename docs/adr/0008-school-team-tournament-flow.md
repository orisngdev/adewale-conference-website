# School-Team Tournament Flow

The competition is modelled around school-team Participants: approval turns a Registration into a Participant, and the school's team advances through edition-specific stages while Reps remain roster members for scores, certificates, and Individual Awards. Grand Finale is a phase, not a single stage; Round of 24 is optional per Edition, face-offs are tie-breakers, and wildcards are advancement reasons rather than stages so historical editions and future event formats can vary without changing the core model.
