# 0004 — Edition-scoped rosters/assignments/progress; evergreen content

A Student roster, Learning-Plan assignments, and Progress are all scoped to a single **Edition** (the current one); a Coordinator never sees or assigns past-edition Students. **Content** is treated differently: questions and study packs are *evergreen* (the edition year is only a tag/filter), and Learning Plans are per-edition but **clonable** from a prior edition or an admin template.

## Considered options
- **Edition-scope people/assignments/progress, keep content evergreen (chosen).** Prevents last year's students appearing in this year's assignment, while letting good material be reused instead of re-uploaded.
- **Everything edition-locked.** Forces re-uploading all content every year.
- **Everything evergreen.** Mixes past cohorts into a current assignment.
